## 🎨 Detailed Specifications by Section

### 1. Main Hero Slider (`premium_hero_slider`)
- **Location**: Top of the homepage, below the navigation bar.
- **Recommended Size**: **1920 × 650 pixels** (High-Definition Retina ready).
- **Aspect Ratio**: **~3:1** (Panoramic wide).
- **Format**: **WebP** (preferred) or **JPG** (82% compression).
- **Design Guidance**:
  - Keep important text, badges, and call-to-action buttons in the **central safe zone** (within the middle 1200px width).
  - Background edges will gently bleed on ultra-wide screens, while mobile screens will scale down proportionately.

---

### 2. Flash Sale Spotlight Banner
- **Location**: Left side card in the Flash Sale section (behind the live countdown timer).
- **Recommended Size**: **500 × 600 pixels** (or 450 × 540 px).
- **Aspect Ratio**: **5:6** (Slight vertical rectangle).
- **Format**: **WebP** or **JPG**.
- **Design Guidance**:
  - Leave breathing room in the middle/upper third for the countdown circle clock overlay.
  - Vibrant promotional imagery (e.g. sale percentage badges, product collection showcases) works best.

---

### 3. Product Banners Slider (`home-product-banner`)
- **Location**: 4-column sliding product promotional cards across the middle of the homepage.
- **Recommended Size**: **1200 × 1200 pixels** (Minimum 600 × 600 px).
- **Aspect Ratio**: **1:1 (Perfect Square)**.
- **Format**: **WebP** or **JPG**.
- **Design Guidance**:
  - Keep content balanced in the square frame.
  - Linked directly to featured products or deals.

---

### 4. RFQ / Sourcing Mid-Page Banner (`mid_page_carousel`)
- **Location**: Full-width banner carousel above the skyscraper/best sellers section.
- **Recommended Size**: **1920 × 630 pixels** (Standard full-width billboard banner).
- **Aspect Ratio**: **~3:1**.
- **Format**: **WebP** or **JPG**.
- **Design Guidance**:
  - Excellent for B2B sourcing banners, seasonal campaigns, or partner promotions.
  - Text should be large and readable with strong contrast against the background.

---

### 5. Skyscraper Spotlight Banner (`sidebar_spotlight`)
- **Location**:
  - **Desktop**: Vertical sidebar banner (left of "Best Seller Products").
  - **Tablet**: Displayed as 3 clean side-by-side columns.
  - **Mobile**: Single full-width card scaled to screen width without cropping.
- **Recommended Size**: **484 × 1120 pixels** (Native display is 242 × 560 px; 2x resolution ensures razor-sharp text on 4K/Retina screens).
- **Aspect Ratio**: **1 : 2.31** (Tall vertical skyscraper).
- **Format**: **WebP** or **JPG**.
- **Design Guidance**:
  - Vertical layout with top-to-bottom hierarchy: Brand Logo / Headline at top, Product Hero in middle, CTA / Offer at bottom.
  - Because height is responsive, all content from the very top to bottom is preserved without cropping.

---

### 6. Featured Square Ad Blocks (`featured_ad_blocks`)
- **Location**: 2 to 3 featured blocks above the wide promotional banner.
- **Recommended Size**: **1200 × 1200 pixels** (Minimum 600 × 600 px).
- **Aspect Ratio**: **1:1 (Perfect Square)**.
- **Format**: **WebP** or **JPG**.
- **Design Guidance**:
  - These blocks display side-by-side on tablet/desktop and stack cleanly on mobile.
  - Use high-contrast creative designs (e.g. Cosmetics & Personal Care, Organic Spices, Specialty Teas).

---

### 7. Middle Horizontal Banner
- **Location**: Full-width container banner under the Featured Square Ad Blocks.
- **Recommended Size**: **1500 × 500 pixels**.
- **Aspect Ratio**: **3:1 (Horizontal Panorama)**.
- **Format**: **WebP** or **JPG**.
- **Design Guidance**:
  - Spans the entire container width with rounded corners.
  - Ideal for brand storytelling, bank card discount promos, or free delivery announcements.

---

### 8. Bottom Showcase Slider (`bottom_showcase_slider`)
- **Location**: Large horizontal slider above the "Most Trusted Brand Partners" section.
- **Recommended Size**: **1920 × 650 pixels**.
- **Aspect Ratio**: **~3:1**.
- **Format**: **WebP** or **JPG**.
- **Design Guidance**:
  - Maximum visual impact for major e-commerce promotions, category spotlights, or marketplace highlights.

---

### 9. Product Card Thumbnails (All Sections)
- **Applicable Sections**:
  - Best Seller Products
  - Featured Products
  - New Arrivals
  - Flash Sale Product Carousel
  - Related Products
- **Recommended Size**: **800 × 800 pixels** (Minimum 600 × 600 px).
- **Aspect Ratio**: **1:1 (Strict Square)**.
- **Format**: **WebP**, **JPG**, or **PNG** (if transparent or white background).
- **Design Guidance**:
  - Center product on clean white (`#FFFFFF`) or transparent background.
  - Leave ~10% padding around the product edges inside the square so it doesn't touch the borders.

---

### 10. Brand Partner / Top Seller Logos
- **Location**: "Most Trusted Brand Partners" carousel at the bottom.
- **Recommended Size**: **400 × 400 pixels** (Rendered as 120 × 120 px circular badge).
- **Aspect Ratio**: **1:1 (Square canvas for circular crop)**.
- **Format**: **PNG (Transparent background)** or **WebP** with white background.
- **Design Guidance**:
  - Keep logo centered inside a circular boundary so text or symbols are not clipped by the circular mask.

---

### 11. Category Sidebar Icons
- **Location**: Left category navigation menu on homepage.
- **Recommended Size**: **64 × 64 pixels** (Rendered at 32 × 32 px).
- **Aspect Ratio**: **1:1 (Square)**.
- **Format**: **PNG (Transparent background)** or **SVG**.
- **Design Guidance**:
  - Simple, minimalist icon with clear silhouette.

## ⚡ Client Image Optimization Checklist

Before uploading any banner or image to the admin panel, verify:

1. **Format Priority**:
   - Use **WebP** whenever possible for 30–50% smaller file sizes with equal quality.
   - Use **PNG** only when transparent backgrounds are required (logos, icons).
   - Use **JPG** (saved at 80–85% quality) for photos without transparency.
2. **Color Profile**:
   - Always export in **sRGB** (not CMYK or Adobe RGB). CMYK will cause washed-out or inverted colors in web browsers.
3. **Resolution**:
   - Set resolution to **72 DPI** (or 96 DPI). 300 DPI is only for print and creates unnecessarily heavy file sizes on websites.
4. **File Size Limits**:
   - Hero / Billboard Banners: Keep under **250 KB**.
   - Square Ad Banners / Spotlight: Keep under **180 KB**.
   - Product Photos: Keep under **120 KB**.
   - Logos & Icons: Keep under **50 KB**.
5. **Recommended Free Compression Tools**:
   - [TinyPNG / TinyJPG](https://tinypng.com) (Bulk PNG and JPG lossless compression)
   - [Squoosh.app](https://squoosh.app) (Google WebP conversion and instant preview)
